# Monetization Strategy: MXit 2.0

## 1. Transaction Fees (Fintech Layer)
- **P2P Transfers**: Free for standard users to encourage adoption.
- **Merchant Payments**: 1.5% - 2.5% fee charged to businesses when users pay via Masheleni QR.
- **Bill Payments**: Convenience fee or commission split with service providers (airtime, electricity).

## 2. Mini-App & Ecosystem Commissions
- **Digital Goods / Gaming**: 30% take rate on in-game purchases (Moola) within MXit Arcade and MoonBase.
- **Real-world Services**: 5-10% lead generation or transaction fee for ride-hailing and food delivery mini-apps.

## 3. MXit Premium (Subscription)
- Target: Power users and community leaders.
- Cost: ~R49 / month.
- Perks: Exclusive profile badges, animated avatars, larger file upload limits, custom AI personas, and advanced chat room moderation tools.

## 4. Business Accounts & Advertising
- **Verified Business Accounts**: Monthly SaaS fee for access to the CRM dashboard and AI chatbot automation.
- **Native Advertising**: Sponsored messages in public chat rooms, sponsored Mini-Apps in the Hub carousel, and branded stickers/GIFs.

## 5. The Creator Economy
- **Paid Communities**: Chat room admins can charge a monthly Masheleni subscription for access to premium content. MXit takes a 10% platform fee.
- **Tipping**: Users can tip creators in live audio/stage channels using Masheleni.
