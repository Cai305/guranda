# Roadmap to 10 Million Users: MXit 2.0

## Phase 1: MVP Launch (Months 1-3)
**Goal: Validate core loop and acquire first 100k users.**
- Focus entirely on blazing-fast 1-on-1 messaging and group chats.
- Target university campuses and tech-savvy youth in South Africa.
- Launch "Invite-a-Buddy" viral loops.

## Phase 2: Masheleni Integration (Months 4-6)
**Goal: Monetize engagement and reach 1M users.**
- Roll out Masheleni 2.0 XRPL wallet.
- Enable P2P transfers and airtime/data purchases (high frequency, low friction).
- Airdrop small amounts of Masheleni to early adopters to bootstrap the economy.

## Phase 3: The Mini-App Explosion (Months 7-12)
**Goal: Increase daily active usage (DAU) and reach 3M users.**
- Open the Developer SDK.
- Launch MXit Arcade and the new MoonBase 2.0 to drive massive engagement.
- Partner with local businesses for food delivery and ride-hailing mini-apps.

## Phase 4: Government & Enterprise (Months 13-18)
**Goal: Become an essential utility, reaching 6M users.**
- Integrate municipal services (pay electricity, report potholes).
- Launch Business Accounts for verified customer support.
- Push into neighboring SADC countries (Namibia, Botswana, Zimbabwe).

## Phase 5: The African SuperApp (Months 19-24)
**Goal: 10 Million Active Users and Pan-African Expansion.**
- Scale infrastructure via multi-region Kubernetes deployments.
- Launch AI-powered translation to break down language barriers across the continent (Swahili, French, Amharic).
- Partner with telcos for zero-rated data access to MXit.
